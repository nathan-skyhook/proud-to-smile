### 4.1 Cleaning (`/service/cleaning`)
> "Dental cleanings are a critical part of your oral health care. At Proud to Smile Dentistry in Bernardsville, New Jersey, Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD, Karima West, RDH, and the team provide thorough cleanings and check-ups to prevent decay and gum disease, promoting a healthy smile."

**Why cleanings matter:** "Your hygienist is extremely detailed when using specialized tools to reach areas, like your gumline and between your teeth, that regular brushing and flossing miss." Process = plaque/tartar removal, flossing, polishing. Untreated plaque/tartar → inflamed gums, tooth decay; poor oral health linked to systemic conditions including heart disease.

**What happens during a cleaning:** Up to one hour; comprehensive exam precedes cleaning; "Dr. Swaintek-Lamb is gentle but effectively breaks away stubborn plaque and tartar." High-powered rotating brush polish finishes the visit; preventive-care education provided.

**Frequency:** Twice yearly (six-month intervals); more often for patients with gum disease or decay-prone teeth.

### 4.2 Veneers (`/service/veneers`)
> Veneers "transform your smile and mask minor oral imperfections, like chips, stains, or gaps between teeth." Dr. Swaintek-Lamb develops personalized treatment plans with step-by-step guidance.

**What are veneers:** "thin porcelain shells that Dr. Swaintek-Lamb bonds to the front surfaces of your teeth." Patients receive a "trial smile" preview before committing.

**Addresses:** chipped teeth, gaps, stains/discoloration resistant to whitening, smaller-than-average teeth, pointy teeth. Practice recommends "six and eight" veneers for optimal balance.

**Procedure:** exam + X-rays, trial period, tooth preparation under local anesthetic, enamel removal, impressions sent to lab, then bonding + bite adjustment.

**Longevity:** "Veneers last 12-15 years on average" with proper maintenance (brushing, flossing, biannual visits); avoid nail biting and chewing ice.

### 4.3 Teeth Whitening (`/service/teeth-whitening`)
> "If you have yellow or discolored teeth, Opalescence™ teeth whitening can eliminate years of stains and help you achieve a more pristine smile." In-office treatment ~2 hours; results last up to 3 years.

**What it is:** "Teeth whitening is a cosmetic treatment that uses hydrogen peroxide gel or other similar solutions to remove stains from the surfaces of your teeth." Office uses Opalescence chemically-activated gel.

**Why teeth discolor:** natural aging/enamel wear revealing yellow dentin; coffee, red wine, cola; tomato sauces, berries, curries; tetracycline medication; poor oral hygiene; tobacco use; oral trauma.

**Treatment:** protective covering applied, then "Opalescence BOOST® whitening gel to the surfaces of your teeth with a handheld brush" — oxidation lightens stains, no special light required.

**Results last:** "an average of three years," maintained via twice-daily brushing, regular flossing, six-month checkups.

### 4.4 Emergency Dentistry (`/service/emergency-dentistry`)
> The practice "make[s] an effort to leave room in their schedule to take care of patients who need immediate assistance" — chipped teeth, broken restorations, sudden pain; immediate appointments and online booking available.

**What it is:** care for unexpected dental injuries/pain (falls, sports injuries) — addresses "sudden pain, soft tissue swelling, or uncontrollable bleeding," can prevent complications or save teeth.

**Appointment:** X-rays + comprehensive exam; treatment may include immediate extraction/oral surgery or temporary fix + follow-up.

**Seek care for:** knocked-out tooth, loose restoration, cracked/chipped teeth, objects stuck between teeth, unusual gum swelling/abscess, severe tooth pain.

**Prevention:** wear protective gear during sports, avoid chewing hard objects, don't use teeth as tools.

### 4.5 Crowns (`/service/crowns`)
> "Dental crowns protect a weak or irregular tooth but preserve the natural root and structure to help you maintain optimal oral health."

**What they are:** "Dental crowns are a type of restoration. They act like caps that replace the visible portion of your natural tooth." Materials: porcelain-fused-to-metal or all-porcelain.

**Why you'd need one:** protect worn-down teeth, complete root canal procedures, cover weakened teeth from prior treatment, stop damage to chipped/cracked teeth, improve discolored tooth appearance, top dental implants.

**Process:** assessment, tooth prep with numbing, impressions, temporary crown, final crown bonding. Maintenance: "brush twice daily, floss, and get regular professional cleanings."

### 4.6 Invisalign (`/service/invisalign`)
> "Invisalign® treatment provides a discreet alternative to traditional metal braces." Offered to teens and adults; may reduce sleep apnea symptoms or TMJ dysfunction.

**What it is:** clear plastic aligners worn 20–22 hours/day (removed only for eating/oral hygiene) that straighten teeth and improve oral/airway health.

**Treats:** crowding, overlapping/crooked teeth, gaps, misaligned jaws, TMJ dysfunction, bite abnormalities (crossbite, underbite, overbite).

**Process:** dental records review, oral exam, digital X-rays, intraoral scanner impressions, computer-aided treatment planning, custom aligner manufacturing, multi-week follow-ups for progressive aligner sets.

**Duration:** "Most people complete Invisalign treatment in 12-18 months*, but depending on the issues addressed and your goals, it may take longer."

**Care:** rinse with cold water multiple times daily; brush nightly with soft bristles; Invisalign Cleaning Crystals available for deeper cleaning.

### 4.7 Dental Implants (`/service/dental-implants`)
Led by Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb and Karima West; implants replicate natural teeth and "can endure a lifetime with proper care."

**What they are:** titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone as an artificial tooth root, supporting a crown that restores visible tooth structure.

**Benefits:** comfortable fit, natural appearance, cost-effectiveness, permanence, easy hygiene. Stimulates bone growth/density, preventing facial sagging and shifting of adjacent teeth.

**Candidacy:** good overall health, non-smoker, adequate jawbone density, healthy gums; adults only.

**Process:** multiple visits over several months — surgical placement, healing/osseointegration period, then crown or bridge placement.

### 4.8 Botox (`/service/botox`)
Positioned as a solution for facial discomfort and jaw pain — treats teeth grinding, headaches, jaw-related issues.

**What it is:** "an injectable drug that's derived from a toxin produced by the bacterium Clostridium botulinum" that reduces muscle activity.

**Dental applications:** teeth clenching/grinding, abnormally large jaw muscles, gummy smile appearance, lip deformities, jaw muscle spasms.

**Highlighted uses:** improving gummy smiles and dental "black triangle" spaces; potential use alongside dental implants to reduce jaw muscle tension; treatment of tension-related headaches.

**Provider:** Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD.

### 4.9 Dentures (`/service/dentures`)
Provided by Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD and Karima West, RDH.

**What they are:** "Dentures are an option to replace one or more missing permanent teeth." Modern dentures = hard resin prosthetic teeth attached to a supporting structure that suctions to the gumline.

**When needed:** most necessary when teeth are severely decayed or gum disease is severe (alternatives like implants/bridges may be better for limited tooth loss).

**Types:** Conventional (removable, upper/lower/both arches); Immediate (temporary, placed day of extraction); Implant-supported (attach to titanium posts in jawbone).

**Care:** "Soak these dentures in a cleaning solution or water overnight." Use soft brushes with non-abrasive denture cleaners; avoid dropping or chewing hard objects.

### 4.10 Periodontal Therapy (`/service/periodontal-therapy`)
> "Consider periodontal therapy if you're one of the more than 45% of American adults with gum disease. At Proud to Smile Dentistry in Bernardsville, New Jersey, Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD, Karima West, RDH, and the team, regularly use periodontal therapy to manage gum disease, including gingivitis and periodontitis."

**What it is:** "a dental subspecialty that treats gum disease, including gingivitis and periodontitis. Without intervention and treatment, gum disease may increase your risk of more serious oral health problems, including tooth loss and bone decay."

**Symptoms:** persistent bad breath, gums pulling away from teeth, painful chewing, loose teeth, sensitive teeth, tender/bleeding gums.

**Treatment types:**
1. Scaling and root planing — "uses handheld tools or a dental laser to clean bacteria and tartar from beneath your gumline"
2. Antibiotic therapy — prescription meds eliminating harmful bacteria
3. Flap surgery — tissue graft procedure for severe gum disease

**Prevention:** "practice good oral hygiene...brushing your teeth twice daily, flossing regularly, and visiting the dentist twice a year"

### 4.11 Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry (`/service/cosmetic-and-restorative-dentistry`)
Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD and Karima West, RDH offer cosmetic dentistry to address gaps, discoloration, and misalignment.

**What it is:** upgrading "worn, damaged, misaligned, and discolored teeth" — also improves functionality and makes teeth easier to clean.

**Common treatments:** gum recontouring (evens gumline), porcelain veneers, dental crowns, dental bonding (tooth-colored resin repairs), tooth-colored fillings, Invisalign® and Spark® clear aligners, porcelain inlays and onlays, Opalescence® teeth whitening.

**Restorative angle:** treats "damaged or missing teeth to improve your smile's overall health and appearance" through fillings, implants, bridges, and crowns.

### 4.12 Extractions & Oral Surgery (`/service/extractions-oral-surgery`)
Dr. Swaintek-Lamb offers advanced oral surgery training.

**What it is:** "Oral surgery removes decay, disease, and damage, which helps you avoid complications and pain in the long term."

**When needed (4 scenarios):**
1. Tooth extractions — advanced decay, prep for implants/dentures, or impacted/crowding wisdom teeth; simple (elevators/forceps) to surgical (gum incisions).
2. Dental implants — "titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots."
3. Periodontal surgery — advanced gum disease, bacterial removal, possible gum contouring.
4. Emergency/biopsy procedures — minor oral surgery for suspicious tissue samples.

**Recovery:** "Recovery from oral surgery is typically faster than from other surgical procedures." Post-care instructions, prescribed meds, soft-food diet, avoid straws and high-impact activity.

### 4.13 Pediatric Dentistry (`/service/pediatric-dentistry`)
Dr. Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD and Karima West, RDH offer cleanings, sealants, digital X-rays for children/teens.

**What it is:** "personalized oral health care to infants, children, and teens" — checkups, preventive cleanings, patient education.

**Services offered:**
- Cleanings — "removes plaque from your child's teeth and beneath their gums" every six months
- Digital X-rays — "capture photos of your child's tooth roots, jaws, and alveolar bone"
- Sealants — "thin plastic coverings...applied to the chewing surfaces"
- Fluoride — "naturally-occurring compound that helps strengthen the tooth enamel"
- Invisalign® — corrects alignment problems
- Periodontal therapy — scaling/root planing for gum disease
- Restorations — fillings or crowns for cavities/chips

**Bring to appointment:** dental insurance card, medication/supplement list, recent X-ray results, questions/concerns, symptom documentation, previous dentist info (if applicable).

### 4.14 Sleep Apnea Treatments (`/service/sleep-apnea`)
> "If you snore in your sleep and wake up feeling tired, you may have a sleep breathing disorder. Proud To Smile Dentistry offers treatments that may help minimize these symptoms and give you the ability to sleep soundly again."

Dr. Patti Swaintek-Lamb and team provide "the latest diagnostic testing and sleep apnea treatments with a focus on airway health" in Bernardsville, NJ.

**Definition:** "Sleep apnea is a condition where complications in your airway and sinus passage cause sleep disruption."

**Symptoms:** snoring, trouble breathing, fatigue, chronic sleep deprivation, memory loss, confusion, acid reflux, weakened immune system, high blood pressure, heart problems.

**Health effects:** increased blood pressure, chronic headaches & fatigue, increased risk of stroke/heart attack, insufficient oxygen to brain, increased risk of mood disorders (e.g. depression).

**Dental treatment approach:** "dentists who specialize in sleep apnea can help treat some of the underlying cause of the condition" — comprehensive care addressing teeth, muscles, and joints of the head and neck.
